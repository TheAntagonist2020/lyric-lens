# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-11)

First release.

- `close-read`: a line-cited reading of one song, with confidence labels, at least 2 readings for each ambiguous line, and a tiered check against sources.
- `challenge`: tests a reader's own interpretation against the text and gives a verdict.
- `catalog`: tracks a motif across an artist's songs, in release order.
- References: a report template, a craft toolkit with genre notes, and a guide to sources and evidence.
