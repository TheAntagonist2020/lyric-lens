# Report template

Use this shape for a full reading. Keep each section tight. Leave out a section that has nothing real in it.

```markdown
## "<Song>" by <Artist> (<year>)

*Line numbers count from the first line you pasted. Blank lines and labels such as [Chorus] are not counted.*

### Thesis
<2-3 sentences: what the song does or argues, not its topic.>

### Motifs and metaphors
- **<image or motif>** (L3, L11, L27): <where it starts, how it changes, what the change means.> [Inference]
- ...

### Voice, tone, and perspective
<Who speaks to whom in each section. Where the tone turns. How the hook changes on repeat. Cite lines.>

### Sound and form
- <Device, with its line, and what it does to the meaning.> [Text]
- ...

### The ending
**L<n>, "<a few words>"**
- **Reading A:** <...> Support: L.., L..
- **Reading B:** <...> Support: L.., L..
- **The text leans toward:** <A or B, or "both, deliberately">, because <line-level reason>.

### Context check
- <What a source says.> (<source>, tier <A/B/C>) [Context]
- <Where the text and the stated intent agree, or part.>

### Open questions
1. <A line worth arguing about, and why.>
2. ...

*Disagree with a reading? Test your own with `/lyric-lens:challenge <your reading>`.*
```
